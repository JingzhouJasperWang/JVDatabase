What the database shows

JV activity is concentrated in strategic sectors.

Automotive is the largest sector with 104 events, followed by Energy with 65 and Aerospace / Defense with 46. Cross-border structures are the norm: 393 of 460 events are cross-border.

Passive participation is scarce. Only 46 events and 48 partner-role rows are flagged as passive/financial.
Passive events cluster by sector. Digital infrastructure accounts for 20 of 46 passive/financial events.
Most partners are operating participants. Active/Operating and Shared/Active roles represent 935 of 1,074 partner-role rows.
Ownership is often balanced. Equal or 50:50 structures appear in 215 of 460 events.

Feature importance

Sector is the strongest predictor in both models.

Importance is measured as the drop in holdout average precision after randomly permuting a feature. Larger drops mean the model relied more on that feature.

The result is clear: passive partner prediction is not driven mainly by cross-border status or deal value. It is driven by sector, then by ownership, geography, and time features.
